## ClaimForge Brand Guidelines

### Name
ClaimForge  
Always written with a capital C and F.

### Logo
- Use the official logo only.
- Do not stretch or recolor the logo.
- Maintain clear space around the logo.

### Colors
Primary palette:
- Dark background tones
- Cyan / electric blue accents

### Usage
- Do not create derivative logos.
- Do not misrepresent affiliation.
